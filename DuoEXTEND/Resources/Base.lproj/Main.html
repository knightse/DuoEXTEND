DuoEXTEND

More room for the web.

Set up in Safari

  1. Enable the extension In Settings, open Apps → Safari → Extensions → DuoEXTEND.
  2. Allow access to a website Open a page in Safari and choose DuoEXTEND in the page menu.
  3. Choose your page width Select Off, Slightly wider, or Full screen in DuoEXTEND’s Safari panel. Your choice is saved for each website.

Settings stay on your device.

Results vary by website. Fixed controls may keep their original width.
